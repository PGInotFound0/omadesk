What is the token?

omadesk prints a QR + URL on the desktop. The part after # in that URL is the token — it authorizes this very browser and stays on this device.

Installing as an app

Open in Chrome → menu → Add to Home screen. Over the internet use Tailscale (this desktop runs it) and open http://<tailscale-host>:9983/<#token>.
